# 3–5 minute demo script

**0:00 — Set the scene**  
“This is THE INSIDER, a conversational investigation game. A confidential employee-data file was opened at 10:47 PM. Four people may know something, but they each have their own perspective. The case engine controls the truth; the characters cannot invent evidence.”

**0:30 — Natural conversation**  
Select Ravi's portrait in the suspect rail and type: *Where were you around 10:40 last night?*  
“Notice that I'm not selecting a predefined dialogue option. I'm asking the NPC naturally. Ravi gives a guarded answer because he has not earned trust and I have not shown him evidence.”

**1:00 — Different knowledge and trust**  
Select Sarah and ask: *Please tell me honestly, what did you see near the server room?*  
“This character has different knowledge from Ravi. Sarah saw him near the server room with a USB, but she does not know what was on it. The respectful approach changes her trust and unlocks her witness statement.”

**1:40 — Evidence changes the game**  
Select Alex and ask: *What does the badge reader show, and was anything unusual with the cameras?*  
“The conversation has added the badge record and camera maintenance note to my evidence bag. That evidence now unlocks the server floor. Objectives, progress, trust, and accessible locations update as I play.” Click **SERVER FLOOR** in the movement bar.

**2:20 — Memory and context**  
Return to Ravi and ask: *Your badge was used at 10:42. Why did you copy the file?*  
“Now I've discovered new evidence, so I'm returning to the same NPC. Notice how the response has changed because the game state changed. Ravi recognizes the badge evidence and reveals why he copied the file. His trust and remembered questions shape what he is willing to say.”

**3:00 — Reveal the motive**  
Ask Mark: *Why were you pushing the employee-data export?*  
“Mark has his own knowledge and deflects responsibility. The story is more nuanced than ‘who touched the server’: Ravi copied the file, while Mark planned to sell the data.”

**3:40 — Resolve**  
Click *Make an accusation*, choose Ravi, and close the case.  
“This demonstrates that the NPC isn't just generating dialogue — it is connected to the game. Evidence gathered through conversation determines whether I have enough to accuse someone, and the backend checks that accusation against the canonical case.”

**Close**  
“Stage 1 is natural-language NPC interaction. Stage 2 is conversation changing evidence, trust, progression, and outcomes. Stage 3 is character-specific knowledge, memory, personality, and context awareness. OpenAI dialogue is optional; the whole case remains playable in fallback mode.”
